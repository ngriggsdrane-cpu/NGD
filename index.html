Nicholas Griggs-Drane is an impact strategist working at the intersection of culture, celebrity, and conscience. He builds campaigns and philanthropic frameworks that turn talent platforms into engines for lasting change.

With experience spanning Capitol Hill advocacy, foundation infrastructure, brand partnerships, and live activations, Nicholas has delivered work that generates hundreds of millions of media impressions while moving real money to real causes.

He operates across entertainment, sports, fashion, and media — helping talent, agencies, and brands translate influence into impact that matters.
