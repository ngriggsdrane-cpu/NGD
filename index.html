Talent Partnerships Brand Advisory Event Creative Production 360 Strategy Campaign Development

For 10 years, Nicholas has served as Director of Client Impact at WME, developing a practice rooted in talent strategy through the lens of impact and philanthropy. His approach is holistic — utilizing talent platforms, revenue models, and passion areas to generate mass exposure, reach, and measurable results. He has worked with A-list stars and emerging digital talent alike, alongside major brands, international foundations, film and TV studios, podcasters, fashion designers, and models. With experience across the Americas, EMEA, and APAC, Nicholas brings a local market sensibility to global campaigns — leveraging access to some of the loudest microphones in the world for causes that matter.
