The Foundation

ATP tennis star Tommy Paul and creator and entrepreneur Paige Lorenze — engaged and based in Connecticut — launched the Kids Outdoors Foundation in January 2026. The mission: fund access to sports with high financial barriers for kids who don't have the means to get in.

Tennis. Skiing. Horseback riding. These are the sports that build confidence, teach risk, and forge the kind of competitive edge that lasts a lifetime — and they're the sports most likely to price out the families who need them most.

The foundation's model is direct and focused. Rather than building new programs from scratch, it funds pre-existing nonprofits already doing strong work in their communities, amplifying their impact. It also hosts its own events — live "inspiring moments" that introduce kids to the power of sport first-hand, including after-school tennis programs in South Florida, where Paul trains.

The idea came from lived experience. "Sport has really made us who we are today," Paul said, speaking about both their journeys — his in professional tennis, hers building a business from the ground up. "The confidence, the ability to take risk — that competitive edge we carry, it came from being outdoors as kids."
