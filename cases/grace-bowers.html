The Concert

For the third consecutive year, Grace Bowers headlined her annual We All Gotta Live Together benefit concert — a multi-genre music event designed to transform the power of live performance into direct funding for two causes she cares about most: Everytown for Gun Safety and MusiCares.

The 2024 concert featured performances by Flavorflav, Ingrid Andress, Brothers Osborne, Luke Spiller of The Struts, Blu Detiger, and Mason Ramsey, with additional donations from Sheryl Crow and Billy Strings.

The evening's centerpiece was a Gibson guitar signed by all performing artists — a one-of-a-kind piece of music history auctioned to benefit both organizations. Total funds raised exceeded $30,000, with proceeds split between Everytown's grassroots gun violence prevention programs and MusiCares' support for music people in need.
