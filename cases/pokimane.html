The Campaign

Pokimane — one of the world's most-followed gaming content creators, with over 40 million followers across platforms — brought her platform to UNICEF's Play For Every Child campaign. The initiative captures celebrity reflections on the role of play in childhood and development, asking cultural leaders to connect their own stories to the universal right of every child to play.

Pokimane's participation began with her appearance at the UNICEF Gala, where she engaged with UNICEF's global leadership and amplified the organization's reach into the gaming and creator community — an audience UNICEF had not traditionally reached at scale.

For a platform built in gaming — an industry that prizes play above all else — the partnership with Play For Every Child was an authentic extension of Pokimane's values. "Play is how I connect with millions of people every day," she said. "Imagine a world where every child has that same access."
