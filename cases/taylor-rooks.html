The Initiative

Taylor Rooks grew up in Suwanee, within Gwinnett County, Georgia. She attended Peachtree Ridge High School before going on to the University of Illinois and a broadcasting career that has taken her to the biggest arenas in professional sports as an Emmy-nominated journalist and Amazon Prime Video NBA host.

But Gwinnett County never left her. On April 22, 2026, the Taylor Rooks Foundation — in partnership with Undue Medical Debt — announced the erasure of $2,149,889 in un-payable medical debt belonging to 1,805 individuals and families in her hometown.

The initiative came from a deeply personal place. "My baby sister has Type 1 Diabetes," Rooks said. "So I know firsthand just how medical bills can pile up; sometimes it can feel like you're drowning, and it seems like there's nothing you can do about it."

Medical debt, she explained, carries a weight beyond the financial. "It lingers far longer than the illness or injury that caused it," Rooks said. "It creates a kind of financial shame that many carry in silence."

The Taylor Rooks Foundation operates across three pillars: Reach (educational resources, community wellness, direct giving), Relieve (medical debt relief, crisis assistance, essential needs), and Represent (mentorship for aspiring journalists, the Rising Rooks Award for underrepresented voices).
