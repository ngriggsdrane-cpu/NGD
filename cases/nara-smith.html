The Partnership

Nara Smith — the South African-born creator and model whose homesteading and slow-living content has captivated millions — partnered with Erewhon, Los Angeles' iconic wellness grocery and smoothie destination, to launch a limited-edition signature smoothie for Save the Children.

The smoothie partnership translated Nara's wellness-oriented audience into philanthropic action. A portion of every purchase went directly to Save the Children, which works in more than 100 countries to improve the lives of children through education, health, nutrition, and emergency response.

The campaign raised over $30,000 — channeled through one of culture's most recognizable purchases, the Erewhon celebrity smoothie, into one of the world's most trusted children's organizations. An unlikely pairing that worked: wellness culture meets global humanitarian impact.
