The Foundation

Sam Smith — one of the world's most celebrated artists, a Grammy, Oscar, Golden Globe, and BRIT Award winner — built The Pink House Foundation as a direct vehicle for LGBTQ+ grassroots funding. Named for the idea of a home built on radical acceptance, the foundation channels philanthropic infrastructure into organizations already doing transformative work in communities that most need it.

The model: immersive residencies. Rather than writing checks from a distance, Sam Smith's team embedded in two cities — New York City and San Francisco — to identify, partner with, and amplify local LGBTQ+ organizations on the ground. Each residency translated lived-in community knowledge into targeted funding.

The Pink House Foundation represents a new model for celebrity philanthropy — one where global fame becomes a conduit for local, grassroots impact. The foundation's structure was built to be replicable: as Sam's reach grows, the residency model can expand to new cities and new causes.
