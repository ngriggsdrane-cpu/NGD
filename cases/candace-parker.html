The Grants

Candace Parker spent 16 seasons as one of the most dominant forces in WNBA history. After retiring, she was named President of Adidas Women's Basketball — and inducted into the Basketball Hall of Fame in the Class of 2026.

But the work she's most proud of happens off the court. The Candace Parker Foundation, fiscally sponsored by the Entertainment Industry Foundation, runs the Glass Ceiling Grants: a $90,000 initiative investing in youth organizations across five cities — Chicago, New York, Nashville, Atlanta, and Los Angeles.

With Adidas as founding brand partner, the Glass Ceiling Grants uplift, inspire, and support young people at every step of their journey by investing in social impact organizations already doing transformative work in their communities.

The foundation's model reflects Parker's own path: invest where talent lives, not just where it's already visible. The grants recognize that potential is distributed equally — opportunity is not.
