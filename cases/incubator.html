The Program

The WME Fashion Incubator is a free, three-month, in-person development program based in New York City. Since its founding in 2020, it has opened doors in one of fashion's most gatekept industries by directly investing in the next generation of creative and executive talent.

Each cohort selects six creatives and six managers. The creative track covers fashion styling, hair, makeup, photography, and set design. The management track prepares participants to represent talent, models, designers, and content creators at the highest level.

The program is led by Ali Bird, Senior Vice President of Global Talent Strategy and Incubation at WME Fashion. Six development sessions cover the business, craft, and community of building a sustainable career in fashion — with the WME network as the door in.

Access is the core mandate. Applicants need a minimum of two years of adjacent industry experience — not an MFA, not a prestigious internship, not a family connection. Just the work and the will.
