The Experience

Coco Jones — Grammy-nominated R&B artist, actress, and one of the most compelling voices of her generation — partnered with Girls Inc. and Lyft to create transformative concert experiences for young girls across select cities on her Why Not More tour.

The partnership went beyond ticket donations. Girls Inc. participants received VIP access — premium seating, private meet-and-greet sessions with Coco herself, and Lyft-sponsored transportation to ensure logistics never stood between a girl and the experience. Everything was covered.

For many of these young women, it was their first major concert. For some, it was the first time they saw someone who looked like them commanding a stage at that scale. Girls Inc.'s mission — inspiring all girls to be strong, smart, and bold — met Coco's platform at the most electric possible moment: live music, front row.
