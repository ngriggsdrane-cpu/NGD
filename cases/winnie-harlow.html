The Moment

Winnie Harlow — model, activist, and one of fashion's most distinctive voices — headlined Black Beauty Roster's Creator Summit as its keynote speaker. BBR, the UK-based talent agency dedicated to representing diverse beauty and creative talent, built its Creator Summit as a platform for examining how influence can be leveraged to shift public perception and drive greater inclusion across beauty, fashion, and media.

Harlow's keynote brought her lived experience of breaking into an industry that told her vitiligo disqualified her — and becoming one of its most recognizable faces anyway. Her conversation centered on how influence carries responsibility, especially when your audience spans generations and crosses cultures.

The summit conversation was filmed by Amazon for inclusion in The CEO Club, a docuseries spotlighting diverse female entrepreneurs and leaders. The appearance amplified Harlow's platform for gender equity and diverse female leadership at the intersection of culture, commerce, and community.
