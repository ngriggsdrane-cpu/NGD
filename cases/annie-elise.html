The Campaign

Annie Elise — true crime podcaster and creator of SERIALously — launched a multi-platform partnership with the Child Rescue Coalition to protect children from exploitation on the internet. The CRC develops technology used by law enforcement to find and arrest child predators, and Annie's audience — true crime listeners who care deeply about justice — was a natural fit.

The partnership activated across every touchpoint of Annie's platform: dedicated SERIALously podcast episodes brought the CRC's mission to millions of listeners; live CrimeCon appearances turned casual interest into committed advocacy; custom merchandise turned her audience into walking billboards for child protection; and newsletter features drove ongoing awareness and fundraising.

True crime content often covers the aftermath of exploitation. Annie's partnership with CRC shifted the conversation upstream — to prevention, technology, and the systems being built to stop predators before they can harm children.

"
True crime listeners understand injustice better than anyone. Now it's time to use that energy to prevent it — before it happens to another child.

Annie Elise — SERIALously
